03 / THEORETICAL FOUNDATIONS

01 · OBSERVABILITY

Data Iceberg Theory

Reconstruct an operational, revisable state from accessible evidence instead of treating the transcript as the whole world.

Ψt ≜ Φ(D≤t)D updates Ψ
02 · TEMPORAL VALUE

Movie Evaluation Theory

Judge the trajectory through past conditions, the current event and several plausible futures—not through one isolated frame.

Et ≜ (Pt, Ct, Ft)Ψ updates E and F
03 · DIRECTION

Agency Principle

Attribute value to actions that actively improve the trajectory while sustaining the goal and the world’s constraints.

Δ(a;h) ≈ Q(h,a) − 𝔼Q(h,a)E attributes change to a
UNIFIED MEASUREMENT OBJECTWt = (St, Ψt, Et)→φ(Wt,Dt) = (Jt, Vt, ht, Rt)

First reconstruct what happened; only then judge capability, violation and termination.

DATA LAYER

Construct cases inside a declared scenario space.

Each case specifies an initial scene, role-specific participant information, source records, and sampling metadata.

realism × information sufficiency × interaction purpose
INPUTSources→DESIGNScenario cell→CONDITIONRole views→OUTPUTCase spec
Ψ
LATENT STATE

What can the evidence support?

Ψt is an operational, revisable estimate grounded in Dt, not a claim about private cognition.

E
EVENT CHAIN

How is the interaction moving?

Et separates past, present, and conditional future paths: Advance, Maintain, Degrade, or Collapse.

J
CAPABILITY JUDGMENT

What capability is demonstrated?

Jt applies the ordered L1–L6 hierarchy only after world reconstruction.

h
TERMINATION

Should evaluation continue?

Vt records a violation or red-line decision; ht records the termination decision.
